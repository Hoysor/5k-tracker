Session notes

  • WU = 15 min easy + 4 strides. CD = 10 min easy.
  • Strides: 20 s building to about mile-race speed, relaxed, never a sprint. Walk back.
  • Hill sprints: moderately steep hill, 10 s hard but smooth, walk down. Full recovery, about 90 s or more.
  • 1 km reps: if a rep is more than 5 s/km slower than target, finish it and end the session.
  • Test: first km no faster than test pace, hold through 4 km, race the last km.
